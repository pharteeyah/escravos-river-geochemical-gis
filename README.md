GIS-Based Spatial Assessment of Naturally Occurring Radioactive and Associated Geochemical Elements in Escravos River Sediments

## Project Brief

This project uses Geographic Information System (GIS) techniques to assess the spatial distribution of naturally occurring radioactive and associated geochemical elements in bottom sediments of the Escravos River, Delta State, Nigeria.

The study covers approximately 28.5 km of the Escravos River and consists of nine sediment sampling locations. The elements assessed are Thorium (Th), Uranium (U), Niobium (Nb), Rubidium (Rb), Barium (Ba), and Zirconium (Zr).

The sampling coordinates and laboratory concentration results were prepared as a GIS-ready dataset and imported into QGIS for spatial visualisation and analysis.

 Spatial Question

How do Th, U, Nb, Rb, Ba, and Zr concentrations vary spatially along the Escravos River, and which sampling locations show relatively higher concentrations that may warrant further environmental monitoring?**

 Study Area

The study area is the Escravos River in Delta State, within the Niger Delta region of Nigeria.

Nine sediment sampling locations are included:

1. Jones' Creek Tributary
2. Okerenkoko (Debama Quarters) Water Front
3. Okerenkoko University Jetty Water Front
4. Seitobobor Polo
5. Kurutie
6. Areton
7. Escravos Water Front
8. Benin River Junction
9. Benikrukru Entrance

 Dataset

The project dataset contains:

* Sampling station and location
* Latitude and longitude
* Sampling depth (m)
* Thorium (Th) concentration (mg/kg)
* Uranium (U) concentration (mg/kg)
* Niobium (Nb) concentration (mg/kg)
* Rubidium (Rb) concentration (mg/kg)
* Barium (Ba) concentration (mg/kg)
* Zirconium (Zr) concentration (mg/kg)

The original coordinates were recorded in Degrees, Minutes and Seconds (DMS) and converted to Decimal Degrees for use in QGIS.

Coordinate Reference System: WGS 84 (EPSG:4326)

 Data Source

The sampling coordinates, depths, and elemental concentration values are primary field and laboratory data from the Escravos River sediment study.

The GIS-ready dataset is available in this repository:

`https://github.com/pharteeyah/escravos-river-geochemical-gis/blob/main/escravos_sediment_samples.csv'
